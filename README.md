### Frontend Tech Lead · React · Next.js · TypeScript

I'm a **Frontend Tech Lead** with 4+ years of experience building modern, scalable, and high-performance web applications. I currently lead the frontend development at the **Judiciary of Tucumán (Poder Judicial de Tucumán)**, defining the architecture, standards, and best practices for the team.

I combine architectural decision-making with team mentoring and a strong focus on clean, maintainable code.

- 📦 **Design and maintain** scalable **monorepo** architectures (Turborepo + Bun)

- 👥 **Mentor developers** and drive the adoption of AI tools to speed up development without sacrificing quality
